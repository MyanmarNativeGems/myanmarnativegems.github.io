Zircon is a zirconium silicate and among the oldest minerals on Earth, with individual crystals dated to billions of years old. It is a distinct mineral from cubic zirconia, the lab-made diamond simulant it is sometimes confused with by name alone.

## Color

Untreated zircon typically ranges from a warm honey and brownish yellow to reddish brown, sometimes called jacinth. The blue and colorless zircon seen elsewhere in the trade is almost always produced by heating; we do not carry heated stones.

## Durability

Zircon measures 6 to 7.5 on the Mohs hardness scale and can be brittle at its facet edges, so it is best cleaned by hand rather than in an ultrasonic cleaner.

## Clarity and cut

Zircon has high dispersion, giving a well-cut stone noticeable fire and brilliance. Clean material is common, and cut quality has an outsized effect on how lively a finished stone looks.

## Treatments

Zircon is heat-treated more often than almost any other gemstone, chiefly to produce blue or colorless material. Ours is sold exactly as mined, in its natural warm tones, with no heat.

## Before you buy

Ask about weight, color, and cut quality. Because natural color is central to how we sell zircon, we're glad to confirm treatment status and discuss a stone's particular tone before you decide.
